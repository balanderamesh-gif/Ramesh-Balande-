# Legal Content Publishing Workflow

## Non-negotiable rule
AI-generated text is a draft only. It must not be represented as legally verified until a qualified human reviewer has checked the relevant primary sources and recorded approval.

## Status progression
1. Draft — Not for reliance
2. Source located — Not yet legally verified
3. Under legal review
4. Verified for publication (reviewer + date required)
5. Needs re-check / Update pending
6. Superseded / Archived

## Minimum required fields before publication
- Marathi and English titles (if bilingual)
- Topic and jurisdiction
- Full Act name, section/rule, official source URL
- Relevant commencement/amendment/transitional note
- Each case's full title, court, date, citation, paragraph and subsequent-history check
- Author and reviewer
- Verification and publication dates
- Version number and change log
- Disclaimer

## Review procedure
1. Confirm the legal question and assumed facts.
2. Open the primary Act, rule, Gazette notification, order or judgment.
3. Check commencement, amendments, state-specific rules and transitional provisions.
4. Read the relevant judgment paragraphs and confirm subsequent treatment.
5. Confirm that the summary does not overstate the decision.
6. Cross-check Marathi and English versions for legal meaning.
7. Record reviewer, date, source URLs and any limitations.
8. Publish only after explicit human approval.
9. Record later corrections and preserve a change log.

## Error reports
Treat a credible report of a material legal error as urgent. Temporarily unpublish or add a visible warning while checking. Correct the text and related PDFs/videos, record the change, and avoid silently changing a material legal conclusion.

## No unsupported guarantees
Never promise “zero errors” or imply that daily monitoring itself guarantees legal accuracy. Promise a transparent verification and correction process instead.
